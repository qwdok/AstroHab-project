Dataset: Flight Crew Physiological Data for Crew State Monitoring (NASA Open Data Portal)
URL: https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/flight-crew-physiological-data-for-crew-state-monitoring
Converted on: 2026-11-14 with tools/prepare_csm.py (window 30 s, step 10 s)
Method: heart rate = 60 / median beat interval of ECG peaks (band-pass 5-25 Hz, intervals outside 0.4-1.5 s rejected,
  at least 12 clean beats and relative spread <= 0.25 per window); breathing = peaks of the R signal (band-pass 0.1-0.7 Hz);
  GSR = window mean of the raw signal, and the same divided by the median GSR of that subject.
Nothing was added by the tool: no health limits and no outside values.
